[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261122/Riodisyaaa/news-image-20261122/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)

A newspaper front page that sums up Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image:
35 stories across 9 regions, every one dated that day, with sources named
beside each story.

- **3 lead stories** — COP31 deadlocked over fossil fuels in Antalya,
  Iran strikes a U.S. air base in Kuwait, magnitude 6.9 quake in Peru's
  Andes — each with a drawn illustration panel.
- **Stories 4–13** in a grid with small drawn illustrations.
- **Stories 14–35** as a numbered one-line list.
- A UTC timeline of the day, six stat boxes, a colour-coded region bar,
  a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" world map, a red-bordered DATE CHECK box listing
  what was left out or had not happened yet, and a HOW WE CHECKED footer.
- All illustrations are drawn (SVG), not photographs.

`index.html` is the page; it is rendered to `entry.png` (1024×1024) and
`main.ts` copies it to the output directory.
